A short tour now sets Wymark up with you on first launch, text pasted from
another app or tool can arrive as a change to review instead of as your own
edit, and in a git repository the review tells you who last touched what it
shows. Switching between open tabs is immediate, with many review and editing
fixes besides.

## Highlights

- A five-slide tour opens on the first launch after this update and sets the
  preferences it explains. Settings > General > "Show the tour" opens it again.
- Paste as Incoming Change (Edit menu, the editor's right-click menu, or
  Cmd+Shift+V) pastes text as a change you review rather than as your own
  edit. After an ordinary paste from outside Wymark, you are offered
  "Mark as incoming".
- Pasting from a web page or another app keeps its formatting as markdown, and
  stays plain text when the formatting would add nothing.
- The lane toggle is gone: there is one diff, and what you type in Wymark is
  always yours.
- In a git repository, the review shows who last touched a change and who
  removed deleted text, and names the repository and branch.
- Switching to a recently viewed tab is immediate.
- The formatting toolbar is on by default, and Wymark has a new icon.
- Fixes to review cards, the comments gutter, keeping your place when text
  reflows, and the outline.
