Fixes the update dialogs. In 0.1.1 and 0.1.2 the offer of a new version, and
the answer to Check for Updates..., never appeared: the page dropped the
shell's message and came straight back, so nothing was shown. Neither of those
versions can update itself, which is why this one has to be installed by
download.

## Highlights

- "Update available", "You're up to date" and "Couldn't check for updates"
  now appear when they should, at launch and from Check for Updates....
- Install this version from wymark.app/download; 0.1.1 and 0.1.2 cannot offer
  it to you.
- No other user-visible changes since 0.1.1.
