In a git folder, the Files panel now marks the files that changed and can
show only those, measured against what you pick. Option+Arrow moves every
block a selection reaches, outside edits show up in milliseconds (also in
folders with non-ASCII names), and the welcome tour is four slides.

## Highlights

- The Files panel marks changed files with their added and removed lines.
  The "changed" switch filters the tree to them, and "vs" picks what
  they are measured against. A deleted file shows while filtering and opens
  its last committed text. Search and reveal follow the filter.
- Cmd+Ctrl+Down and Cmd+Ctrl+Up walk the changed files, and so do the arrows
  on the status chip's file row.
- Option+Arrow moves every block the selection reaches, not only the one at the
  caret. A move keeps comment markers with their block and leaves lists as
  the file had them.
- Outside edits reach an open document in milliseconds instead of half a
  second, and they now reach documents in folders with non-ASCII names.
- A folder or document opened through a symbolic link says so, and says
  where to open it from to compare with git.
- The welcome tour is four slides, with pictures of the toolbar.
- Fixed: a moved paragraph pairs as a move in more cases (inside a quote,
  past a short neighbour, beside another change); a shown tooltip follows its
  text; the Help menu's GitHub item shows its ampersand.
- Security update to the app's built-in web server (Bandit 1.14.0).
