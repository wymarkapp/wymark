Zoom gets two writing modes: Focus dims everything but the block you are
in, and Typewriter keeps your line at one height while the page moves under
it. When another program writes into a document you have open, Wymark now
merges the change in place instead of keeping two copies of a paragraph or a
list. The welcome document is rewritten, and from now on an update that
changes it replaces your copy.

## Highlights

- Focus (`F8` or `⌘D`) and Typewriter (`F9` or `⌘T`) in zoom. Zoom also gets a
  left divider, and its outline folds into a dropdown when space runs short.
- An edit from another program to a document you have open merges where it
  belongs. This holds when it replies to a comment, rewrites the commented
  text, resolves the comment, or edits one item of a list. Before, some of
  these kept both versions side by side.
- A new welcome document. When an update changes it, the update replaces
  your copy, along with anything you wrote in it, so keep notes in a document
  of your own.
- Accept or reject the change the status chip names, without going to its
  card.
- Long change cards are clamped and expand in place. Cards may grow wider, up
  to 600px.
- Deleting a whole section shows as one change per heading instead of dozens.
  A run of added blank lines is one change again.
- Fixed: Copy link could fail with a permission error everywhere in the app.
- Fixed: formatting bar icons could disappear until a restart.
- Fixed: a nested outline of headings drifted apart in the Formatted view.
- `⌘,` opens and closes Settings, and the footer hints use the app's tooltip.
