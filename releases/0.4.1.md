You can comment a code block or a picture in the formatted editor, and a comment
on a whole block is marked by a bar at its right edge. The outline shows which
section you are in, and a Wide strip is available in zoom. Several fixes for
undo, footnotes, soft-wrapped text and comments that vanished.

## Highlights

- New: a code block has a Comment button in its corner, and a selected picture
  offers one too. Cmd+Opt+M does the same.
- New: the outline marks the section you are reading, in the side panel and in
  zoom's strip.
- New: zoom's outline strip can be Wide, and the choice is kept.
- Improved: a comment on a whole table, code block, diagram or picture shows as
  a bar at the block's right edge, not a tint or a left border.
- Improved: a picture in a resting comment card comes first, ahead of its
  markdown.
- Fixed: a new comment, typed and closed, could vanish a moment later.
- Fixed: Enter or Backspace in a soft-wrapped paragraph or list item no longer
  folds its wraps.
- Fixed: deleting part of a bare web address or an angle-bracket link strikes
  only what was removed in its change card, not the whole address.
- Fixed: typing a second footnote right after the first no longer lands inside
  the first one's label.
- Fixed: in Pure mode, Backspace on a star inside a code block no longer
  deletes a second one, and stars typed there are kept.
- Fixed: undo after typing a repeated letter could leave the caret one short.
- Fixed: the Settings note about custom heading IDs names Settings > Markdown.
- Security: updates to Phoenix, LiveView, Bandit and the url library.
