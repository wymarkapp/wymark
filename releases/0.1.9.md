Documents now read in your system's own typefaces, with headings sized and
weighted to stand out from the text, and Settings lets you choose a sans or a
serif for the text and for headings. The last character you typed is no longer
occasionally swallowed, and a comment on a few words in a table cell now marks
that cell instead of the whole table.

## Highlights

- The document is set in your system's font (San Francisco on a Mac, Segoe UI
  on Windows), with headings in the same face at a clearer size and weight.
  Settings has two new rows under Theme: Document font (Sans or Serif) and
  Headings (Same as text, Sans or Serif). The serif is your system's own too.
- Fixed: the last character or space you typed could occasionally vanish
  shortly after you typed it.
- A comment inside one table cell marks just that cell, and opening it scrolls
  to the cell rather than to the top of the table. A cell can hold several
  comments.
- Comments are easier to see in dark mode.
