The AI sidecar is now a switch on each document, and while it is on the
document carries a line at its top that tells a tool where the sidecar is. A
file written from outside Wymark is taken exactly as it was written: Wymark no
longer writes a merge of its own back over it, and where the write undid
something of yours a card offers to put it back. Wymark checks for an update
at every launch. The welcome document describes both changes, so this update
replaces your copy of it again.

## Highlights

- The sparkles button in the toolbar turns the AI sidecar on for the
  document you are in, and opens the sidecar file in a tab beside it. It is
  off until you turn it on.
- While the switch is on, the document carries one HTML comment at the top
  that names the sidecar file. Both views show it above the text; it is
  never part of your review, your versions or an export.
- Settings > Review & comments has two new controls: whether enabling the
  sidecar adds the HTML comment, and the comment's text.
- A write from outside Wymark that removes words you added, brings back
  text you deleted, or removes a reply gets a card at the place, with
  `Put back` or `Delete again`, and `Keep`.
- The text from before such a write is kept in the Versions panel under
  **Overwritten** for thirty days, and `⌘Z` takes the write back as one
  step.
- Typing that never pauses reaches the file within about a second, where a
  busy document could go minutes without a write.
- Fixed in the Formatted view: bold typed against an existing bold run
  joins it; toggling bold off after a space leaves no asterisks; deleting
  a heading with its section leaves no hashes; a drag or `⇧`-arrow
  selection crosses a code block; splitting a paragraph and joining it
  again keeps the space.
- Fixed: a space typed just as an outside write landed could be lost, and
  a comment over a whole list item collapsed the item after it.
- A toast counts its eight seconds only while the window has focus.
